Welcome to your new blog. This is a sample post — replace or delete it.

Code blocks tagged `python:run` execute live when the page loads:

```python:run
print("Hello from your new blog!")
```

Link to other posts by their **file path** under `posts/` — the blog
resolves it to the real URL at render time:

```markdown
[Some post](post/some-post.md)
[An event](events/meetup.md)
```

External URLs (`https://...`) open in a new tab automatically; internal
post links stay in the same tab.
